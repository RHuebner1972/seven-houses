You are illustrating a single card from a cohesive 78-card tarot deck called Seventh House.

Overall style
- Fine-art tarot illustration, painterly but precise, like a modern illuminated manuscript.
- Dark celestial field: deep indigo fading to near-black (#0B0B1F to #05050C).
- Gold line work and accents (#C9A84C), pale-gold highlights (#E8D5A3), parchment skin and cloth (#F2EBDD), muted violet-grey shadows (#9A94B3).
- Quiet, dignified, nocturnal. Astrology-informed, never cartoonish, never photorealistic, never horror, never gore.
- Consistent lighting: a single cool moonlight plus warm gold rim light. The same palette and brush language on every card.

Composition
- Full-bleed portrait, 2:3 aspect (1024×1536). Edge-to-edge painted scene. 4-pixel white margin.
- Paint the card's exact name once on the face, in elegant gold lettering along the bottom edge (centered, readable, small enough not to crowd the figure). Use the same hand-lettered serif style on every card.
- No other text: no roman numeral, no caption, no watermark, no extra letters or numbers, no subtitle.
- No card frame, small ornate border, slight rounded corners, no playing-card overlay.
- One clear focal figure or emblem, centered or slightly above center, with breathing room above the title.
- Backgrounds stay atmospheric (night sky, faint constellations, thin gold geometry, Whole Sign house motifs) so the deck reads as one family.

Iconography
- Major Arcana: archetypal human figures, contemporary-timeless dress, symbolic objects only.
- Wands: fire, living wood, sparks, growth, will.
- Cups: water, vessels, moonlight on liquid, feeling.
- Swords: air, steel, wind, thought, clarity.
- Pentacles: earth, coin-disks engraved with a heptagram, soil, craft, body.
- Recurring deck motif: a seven-pointed star (subtle, never the whole subject except on the card back).
- Reversible card back: non-directional gold star-field on indigo. No figure, no upright orientation.

Avoid
- Photorealism, 3D render look, anime, comic ink, collage, neon cyberpunk.
- Logos, UI chrome, modern devices, watermarks, any writing other than the card name.
- Extra panels, split screens, or "card within a card".
